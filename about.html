Our Story

North Star Bakery was founded in 2009 by third-generation baker Maria Santos and her husband Thomas. What started as a small weekend farmers market stand has grown into a beloved neighborhood institution. Every morning, our bakers arrive before dawn to mix dough, shape loaves, and prepare fresh pastries using time-honored techniques passed down through Maria's family.

We named our bakery "North Star" because, like the guiding star, we're committed to leading our community toward better bread, deeper connections, and a return to the simple pleasures of sharing a meal together. Over 15 years, we've built relationships with our neighbors, local farmers, and suppliers who share our values of quality and sustainability.

Today, North Star Bakery stands as a testament to the power of passion, tradition, and community. We're proud to be more than just a bakery—we're a gathering place where memories are made and traditions are passed down through generations.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that great baking starts with great ingredients. That's why we partner with local farmers and suppliers whenever possible. We're committed to transparency and quality in every ingredient we use:

  • Flour: We source organic, stone-ground flour from Hillside Mills, just 15 miles away. Their commitment to sustainable farming aligns perfectly with our values.
  • Butter: Our pastries are made with imported European butter for authentic flavor and superior quality. We never use margarine or shortening.
  • Fruits & Nuts: Seasonal local produce and responsibly sourced nuts. We work with nearby farms to get the freshest seasonal ingredients.
  • Honey & Sweeteners: Fair-trade and local suppliers. We use real honey, not high fructose corn syrup or artificial sweeteners.
  • Salt & Yeast: Premium sea salt and natural yeast cultures ensure the best flavor profiles in our breads.

We avoid artificial preservatives and additives. What you see on the ingredient list is what we use—nothing more. This means our breads are best enjoyed fresh, usually within 24-48 hours of baking.

Meet Our Bakers

Maria Santos - Head Baker & Founder

Maria brings 35 years of professional baking experience to North Star. Her signature sourdough starter has been alive and thriving since 2009, fed daily with organic flour. Maria comes from a family of bakers stretching back three generations in Portugal.

When she's not in the kitchen, Maria teaches bread-making classes to the community and mentors young bakers. Her philosophy is simple: bake with love, use the best ingredients, and respect the craft.

Thomas Santos - Co-Founder & Business Manager

Thomas handles the business side of things, from sourcing ingredients to community partnerships. He's passionate about creating a welcoming space for everyone who walks through our door and ensuring North Star remains a neighborhood institution.

Thomas is often the first face customers see in the morning, and he takes pride in knowing our regulars by name and their favorite orders.

James Chen - Pastry Chef

James trained in Paris at a prestigious culinary school and brings European pastry traditions to our kitchen. He's always experimenting with seasonal flavors and creating new treats for our customers while respecting traditional techniques.

James specializes in Danish pastries and croissants, and he's responsible for our award-winning pain au chocolat. He believes pastry is both an art and a science.

Sofia Romero - Assistant Baker

Sofia is an apprentice baker learning the craft from Maria. She's particularly skilled at decorating our custom cakes and creating beautiful presentations for special events.

Sofia started as a customer who fell in love with our breads, and now she's becoming an integral part of our team. She represents the next generation of artisan bakers.

Our Community Commitment

North Star Bakery isn't just a business—it's part of the fabric of our neighborhood. We take our role in the community seriously:

  • Food Bank Donations: We donate unsold bread to local food banks at the end of each day, ensuring no food is wasted
  • Educational Workshops: We provide baking workshops for school groups and community organizations, sharing our knowledge with the next generation
  • Local Partnerships: We support local farmers through direct purchasing partnerships, often paying premium prices to support sustainable agriculture
  • Employment Opportunities: We employ neighborhood residents and offer paid apprenticeships to people learning the baking trade
  • Event Sponsorship: We sponsor local events, school fundraisers, and community gatherings by donating baked goods
  • Accessibility: We work hard to make our baked goods accessible to everyone, including gluten-free and vegan options
