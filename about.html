Our Story

North Star Bakery was founded in 2009 by third-generation baker Maria Santos and her husband Thomas. What started as a small weekend farmers market stand has grown into a beloved neighborhood institution. Every morning, our bakers arrive before dawn to mix dough, shape loaves, and prepare fresh pastries using time-honored techniques passed down through Maria's family.

We named our bakery "North Star" because, like the guiding star, we're committed to leading our community toward better bread, deeper connections, and a return to the simple pleasures of sharing a meal together.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that great baking starts with great ingredients. That's why we partner with local farmers whenever possible:

  • Flour: We source organic, stone-ground flour from Hillside Mills, just 15 miles away
  • Butter: Our pastries are made with imported European butter for authentic flavor
  • Fruits & Nuts: Seasonal local produce and responsibly sourced nuts
  • Honey & Sweeteners: Fair-trade and local suppliers

We avoid artificial preservatives and additives. What you see on the ingredient list is what we use—nothing more.

Meet Our Bakers

Maria Santos - Head Baker & Founder

Maria brings 35 years of baking experience to North Star. Her signature sourdough starter has been alive and thriving since 2009. When she's not in the kitchen, Maria teaches bread-making classes to the community.

Thomas Santos - Co-Founder & Business Manager

Thomas handles the business side of things, from sourcing ingredients to community partnerships. He's passionate about creating a welcoming space for everyone who walks through our door.

James Chen - Pastry Chef

James trained in Paris and brings European pastry traditions to our kitchen. He's always experimenting with seasonal flavors and creating new treats for our customers.

Sofia Romero - Assistant Baker

Sofia is an apprentice baker learning the craft from Maria. She's particularly skilled at decorating our custom cakes and creating beautiful presentations.

Our Community Commitment

North Star Bakery isn't just a business—it's part of the fabric of our neighborhood. We:

  • Donate unsold bread to local food banks at the end of each day
  • Provide baking workshops for school groups and community organizations
  • Support local farmers through direct purchasing partnerships
  • Employ neighborhood residents and offer paid apprenticeships
  • Sponsor local events and school fundraisers
